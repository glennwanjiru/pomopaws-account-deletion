Account support

Delete your PomoPaws account

Use this page to request deletion of your account and associated data. You don't need to sign in or reinstall the app.

The current PomoPaws app works without an account. These instructions apply to accounts created in an earlier version.

What happens next

  1. 01

    You send the request

    Email us your registered account address and ask to delete your PomoPaws account and associated data.

  2. 02

    We verify it's you

    We may reply with a verification step to protect your account. You won't need to reinstall the app.

  3. 03

    We complete deletion

    Within 30 days after ownership verification, we delete the account data under our control and confirm by email.

−

What gets deleted

For your old PomoPaws account, we delete the following data under our control, where held:

  • Your authentication account, sign-in email and associated credentials.
  • Your account profile, user identifier and associated cloud records.
  • Focus history, preferences or other app data linked to that account in our cloud storage.

Account deletion is permanent. Data stored only on your phone is separate; clear PomoPaws storage in Android Settings to remove that local data. Images saved to your gallery must be deleted from your gallery separately.

Purchases & subscriptions

Google Play manages your purchase records and subscriptions through your Google account. Deleting a PomoPaws account does not delete your Google account or automatically cancel a subscription.

If you want to stop subscription renewals, cancel your subscription in Google Play separately.

Manage Google Play subscriptions ↗

Google retains its own payment records under its policies. Requests concerning those records must be made to Google.

A note on retained records

We use your request email to verify ownership, process deletion and confirm the result. We retain this correspondence only while handling the request. If a specific record must be retained for a legal obligation, security or a dispute, we will explain what is retained, why and for how long in our reply.
